Track Your Progress

Tracking progress helps you stay motivated and see your improvement over time.

Ways to Track Progress

  1. Take progress pictures every few weeks
  2. Track your workouts
  3. Record your body weight
  4. Stay consistent

Tips to Stay Consistent

  • Set realistic goals
  • Stay patient
  • Follow a routine
  • Do not skip workouts

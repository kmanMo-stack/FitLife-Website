Weekly Workout Plan

This workout plan is designed to help build a lean and muscular physique. It focuses on consistency, controlled movements, and balanced training across all muscle groups.

Weekly Split

  • Day 1 – Back + Biceps
  • Day 2 – Legs
  • Day 3 – Chest + Triceps
  • Day 4 – Active Recovery
  • Day 5 – Back + Shoulders
  • Day 6 – Legs Light + Conditioning
  • Day 7 – Rest

Day 1 – Back + Biceps

  • Dumbbell Rows – 4x10 to 12
  • Lat Pulldown – 4x10
  • Rear Delt Raises – 3x12 to 15
  • Dumbbell Curls – 3x10 to 12
  • Hammer Curls – 3x10
  • Finish with 10 to 15 minutes on treadmill

Day 2 – Legs

  • Goblet Squats – 4x12
  • Bulgarian Split Squats – 3x10 each leg
  • Romanian Deadlifts – 4x10
  • Step-ups – 3x10 each leg
  • Calf Raises – 4x15 to 20
  • Finish with 10 minutes of cardio

Day 3 – Chest + Triceps

  • Dumbbell Bench Press – 4x10
  • Incline Press – 3x10
  • Push-ups – 3 sets
  • Tricep Dips – 3x12
  • Overhead Tricep Extensions – 3x12

Day 4 – Active Recovery

  • 20 to 30 minute walk or light jog
  • Planks – 3 sets
  • Leg Raises – 3x12
  • Russian Twists – 3x15

Day 5 – Back + Shoulders

  • Dumbbell Rows – 4x10
  • Rear Delt Raises – 3x12
  • Shoulder Press – 4x10
  • Lateral Raises – 3x12 to 15
  • Shrugs – 3x12

Day 6 – Legs Light + Conditioning

  • Bodyweight Squats – 3x15
  • Walking Lunges – 3x12 each leg
  • Jump Squats – 3x10
  • Step-ups – 3x10
  • Calf Raises – 3x20
  • Finish with 15 to 20 minutes of cardio

Day 7 – Rest

Day 7 is a full rest day. A light walk is fine, but the main goal is to let the body recover.

Workout Rules

  • Workouts should last around 60 to 75 minutes
  • Rest 60 to 90 seconds between sets
  • Control every rep instead of rushing
  • Stay consistent each week

Nutrition Tips

Nutrition plays a major role in muscle growth and recovery. Eating the right foods consistently will help you perform better in workouts and see results faster.

You do not need a complicated diet. Focus on simple, balanced meals with enough protein, carbs, and healthy fats.

High Protein Foods

  • Chicken – lean and high in protein
  • Turkey – great for meal prep
  • Eggs – affordable and easy to cook
  • Greek yogurt – high protein snack
  • Fish – good for protein and healthy fats

Daily Nutrition Tips

  • Drink plenty of water throughout the day
  • Eat protein with every meal
  • Do not skip meals
  • Keep meals simple and consistent

Sample Daily Meal Plan

Meal Example
Breakfast Greek yogurt, fruit, and granola
Lunch Chicken, rice, and vegetables
Dinner Turkey bowl with potatoes and salad
Snack Boiled eggs or protein shake

Important Tips

Consistency matters more than perfection. You do not need to eat perfectly every day, but staying consistent with your meals will help you build better habits over time.

Focus on eating enough protein, staying hydrated, and keeping your meals balanced. That alone will take you far.
